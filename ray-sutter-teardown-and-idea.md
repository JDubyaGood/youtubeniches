## The idea: "Frank Delaney", scam protection for retirees

**One line:** Ray Sutter's exact format, pointed at the #1 fear retirees have after their health: *being tricked out of their savings.*

### Why this niche
- **Same audience, same emotion.** Seniors, fear, then calm protection. It's the same psychology Ray uses, pointed at something they worry about more often than blackouts.
- **Higher RPM.** Banking, insurance, identity-protection and security advertisers pay more than prepping advertisers do.
- **Endless topics + news hooks.** New scams make the news every week (AI voice-clone "grandparent" calls, Medicare card calls, toll-road texts, gift-card tech support). Each one becomes a new "7 Scams…" video.
- **Ebook sells itself.** A retiree who just learned about a scam wants the checklist, and so do their adult children.
- **Proof it works:** Ray's own robbery video hit 223K, and a small channel ("The Good Steward") pulled 11K views in 2 days on "12 Scams Targeting Seniors That Will Erase Their Savings".

### Persona
- **Name:** Frank Delaney (channel = @FrankDelaney)
- **Look:** AI talking head, late 60s, cardigan or plain button-down, home-study background with a bookshelf. Warm and grandfatherly, not a cop.
- **Voice:** slow, plain, a bit gruff: "Let me be straight with you."
- **Backstory:** keep it honest (see the warning below). A good framing is "I spent my retirement studying how these scams work so you don't learn the hard way." Don't invent a badge.

### Script formula (copy Ray beat-for-beat)
1. **Cold open fear hook (0:00–0:45).** "If your phone rings tonight and it sounds exactly like your grandson crying, I promise you the first thing you'll do is the thing the scammer is counting on…" Tease that "number one isn't the one you've heard about."
2. **Ebook pitch (~0:50).** "I put every script these people use, and exactly what to say back, into one plain guide. It's called *The Hang-Up Handbook*. Scan the code on screen or grab the link in the pinned comment. $37."
3. **Countdown 7→1 (or 15→1).** For each scam: how it starts (the exact words they use), why it works on smart people, the tell, what to do, and a **senior layer** (hearing aids and voice clones, living alone, trusting authority, bank app confusion).
4. **Myth-busting for trust.** "Anybody who tells you caller ID protects you is wrong. Scammers fake it in seconds." Cite FTC, FBI IC3, AARP Fraud Watch numbers.
5. **Soft re-pitch mid-video.** "This is exactly the gap a family safe-word plan closes. It's page 12 of the handbook."
6. **Verdict + bridge to the next item**, with the stakes climbing until number one.

### Digital product
**The Hang-Up Handbook**: $37 PDF
- The 25 most common scam scripts word-for-word, with the exact thing to say back
- Family safe-word setup sheet
- Printable "by the phone" card: 10 things no real bank, Medicare office or IRS agent will ever ask
- A "first 60 minutes after you've been scammed" action plan (who to call, how to freeze credit)
- **Upsell:** "Protect Your Parents" edition aimed at the adult children who find the videos ($47)

### Production stack (faceless)
Script (AI-drafted, fact-checked against FTC/FBI/AARP) → AI voice → AI talking-head avatar for the intro, pitch, verdicts and outro → B-roll (phone screens, text-message mockups, stock of seniors) for the list items → 20–35 min runtime → 5–7 uploads/week to match Ray's pace.

### ⚠️ Rules to not get burned
- **Don't fake credentials.** No "retired FBI agent" or "22 years as a detective" unless a real person with that history is behind it. A fabricated expert persona giving financial and safety advice can get the channel demonetized, and it's deceptive to exactly the vulnerable audience you're serving.
- **Tick YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" box** for a realistic AI face. It's required.
- **Be careful with the scam demo itself.** Show the scam scripts so people recognize them, but never walk through how to *run* one.
