# YouTube Niche Research — September 2026

Source: kloudboard MCP (`find_niches` + `competitor_channel`), pulled 2026-09-24.

## Search filters used

| Filter | Value |
|---|---|
| Subscribers | < 100,000 |
| First upload | on or after 2026-05-24 (last ~1–4 months) |
| Est. monthly revenue | ≥ $7,000 |
| Faceless only | yes |
| Format | long-form (Shorts excluded; every pick averages 16+ min) |

This returned 125 channels. I checked the top videos of shortlisted channels with `competitor_channel` to confirm each has a 200K+ view video. Channels that failed that check were dropped: The Career Chronicles (top video 122K) and Boots & Roses (top video 87K). Frontline Responders was also dropped because it looks like re-uploaded TV content, so it can't be recreated.

## Criteria checklist (need 5 of 7)

1. < 100K subs
2. Started in the last 1–4 months
3. Has a viral video (200K+ views)
4. Growing, not declining (still uploading, recent videos outperform)
5. Recreatable (faceless, script + stock/AI visuals + voiceover)
6. Other small channels blowing up in the same niche
7. BONUS: can be monetized with a digital product

## Caveats

- **The revenue figures are kloudboard estimates.** They are calculated from views × estimated RPM, not taken from real AdSense data.
- **"First upload" dates come from the kloudboard database.** A channel could be older and have been renamed or had old videos deleted. Check the channel's "About → Joined" date before you commit to a niche.
- **Rows 5, 8, 12, 18 and 19** could not be checked video by video because the workspace ran out of AI credits. For these, the 200K+ viral video is inferred from the channel's average and median views. Every other row lists a specific video with its view count.
- **Spo0ky started on 2026-05-25**, which is exactly 4 months ago. It meets the 1–4 month criterion but not the "1–3 months" in the prompt.
- **Ray Sutter uses a narrator persona** ("22 years in the Army"), but there's no on-camera face.
